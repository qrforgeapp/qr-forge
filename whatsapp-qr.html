What is a WhatsApp QR Code?

A WhatsApp QR code is a scannable code that can open a WhatsApp conversation with a specific phone number. It makes it easier for customers, friends, and visitors to contact you without manually saving your number.

How to Create a WhatsApp QR Code

  1. Enter your WhatsApp phone number with the country code.
  2. Add an optional pre-written message.
  3. Click Generate WhatsApp QR Code.
  4. Scan the QR code with a phone.
  5. Download and share your QR code.
