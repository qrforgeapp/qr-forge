Frequently Asked Questions

Is QR Forge free?

Yes. QR generation is free and happens in your browser.

Do you store my QR information?

QR Forge is designed to generate QR data in your browser. Avoid entering highly sensitive information into any online tool.

Can I download my QR code?

Yes. PNG, JPG and SVG download options are provided.

Can I create a WhatsApp QR code?

Yes. Choose WhatsApp and enter the phone number in international format.

Can I use QR Forge on a phone?

Yes. The site is responsive and designed for mobile screens.

Will every QR code work forever?

A static QR code keeps its encoded information, but the destination itself must remain valid. For example, a QR containing a URL stops being useful if that URL stops working.
